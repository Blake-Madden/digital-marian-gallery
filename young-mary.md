<p style="text-align:center;">
  <em>
    Seated upon a gentle cloud beside her mother, Mary studies not scripture alone, but the patterns hidden within the world itself. 
    Open books on observation, number, and design surround her alongside scattered graphs, colored pencils, and hand-drawn reports — the quiet tools of careful attention. 
    The painting reimagines sacred education as an act of curiosity: the disciplined, almost devotional practice of learning how to see clearly.
  </em>
</p>
